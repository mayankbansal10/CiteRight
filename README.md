# 🚗 CiteRight — AI Agent to Automatically Fight Parking Tickets
### 🏆 Third Prize Winner — NVIDIA Hackathon

## Project info

**URL**: https://lovable.dev/projects/ec2e0e63-4c66-4f7e-8318-a901f3036e83

Citeright is an autonomous **AI legal-assistant agent** that helps people **fight parking citation tickets automatically — without a lawyer**.  
Built during the **NVIDIA Hackathon**, this project shows how agentic workflows and legal-reasoning AI can make bureaucratic processes more accessible and fair.

---

## 🌟 What Citeright Does
- ⚖️ **Automated legal reasoning** to analyze the validity of a parking citation  
- 🔍 **Evidence extraction** from local regulations, municipal codes, and signage rules  
- 🤖 **Agentic workflow orchestration** for end-to-end dispute handling  
- 📝 **Auto-generated dispute letters** tailored to city/jurisdiction requirements  
- 🚀 A real example of using GenAI to improve **equitable access** to legal support  

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters
Citeright empowers people to challenge unfair citations **without the cost, complexity, or time** of navigating the legal system.  
It demonstrates the potential of AI to automate complex reasoning loops and bridge the gap between citizens and bureaucratic processes.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (High-Level)
- NVIDIA GMI ecosystem tools  
- LLM-based agentic reasoning  
- Retrieval + document parsing pipeline  
- Structured prompt + reasoning workflows  
- Automated dispute letter generation engine
